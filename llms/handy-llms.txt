# Handy

> Handy (part of Angi / ANGI Homeservices) is a home-services marketplace connecting customers with pre-screened professionals for cleaning, handyman work, furniture assembly, and in-home installations. Its Partner API lets retailers attach fixed-price installation and service bookings to an eCommerce or in-store checkout.

## APIs
- [Handy Partner Orders API](https://partners.services.handy.com/docs/orders_api): Create and manage orders, products, line items, and bookings (Swagger 2.0).
- [Handy Webhooks](https://partners.services.handy.com/docs/webhooks): Booking lifecycle callbacks (rescheduled, completed, cancelled, service fulfilled, job claim assigned).
